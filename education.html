Education

The education required to become an Information Technology Project Manager is fairly lenient.

You can get into this role with 2 pathways, either through a bachelor's degree in IT, or through work experence and certifications.

If you choose to get the bachelor's degree, you end up going to coledge for 4 years to earn your degree.

You can also choose to get there with certifications, a more flexable plan that is harder but takes less time and money.
